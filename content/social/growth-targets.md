# BeMama organic discovery targets

Use this list for slow, human outreach. The goal is to participate in conversations where BeMama can genuinely add value—not to mass-follow, mass-comment, or drop links.

## Guardrails

- Follow public pages only when their audience overlaps with pregnancy, postpartum, feeding, child health, or parenting.
- Read the specific post before commenting; write one useful, non-promotional thought.
- No BeMama link, app pitch, copy-paste comments, hashtags, or unsolicited private messages in public comments.
- Keep the daily limit to 3–5 thoughtful comments and take a rest day if the account starts looking repetitive.
- Do not join private groups or represent BeMama as a medical provider.

## Comment angles

These are starting points only. Rewrite them to match the post.

- **Mental health:** “The reminder that parents should not have to carry perinatal mental-health challenges alone is so important. Peer support can make asking for help feel possible.”
- **Feeding support:** “Encouragement and connection make feeding support feel much less lonely. Practical, judgment-free help can make a real difference.”
- **Pediatric education:** “Clear, calm health information gives parents a better starting point for conversations with their pediatrician. Thank you for making an important topic easier to understand.”
- **Local maternal/newborn care:** “Accessible, trustworthy care matters so much during pregnancy and the newborn stage. Thank you for highlighting the people and services families can turn to.”
- **Parenting education:** “I appreciate the focus on practical support and realistic expectations. Parents need information that helps them take the next small step without shame.”

## Weekly rhythm

- Monday–Friday: 3 useful comments maximum per day, each on a different relevant post.
- Once or twice per week: one genuine collaboration message to a page whose work BeMama already understands.
- End of week: record replies, saves, profile visits, and which topics earned conversation. Keep only the topics that create real responses.
