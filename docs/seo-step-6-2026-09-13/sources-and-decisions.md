## Competitor intent and the actual gap

Huckleberry has dedicated [four-month sleep](https://huckleberrycare.com/blog/4-month-olds-and-sleep) and [six-month feeding](https://huckleberrycare.com/blog/6-month-old-feeding-schedule) pages. The BeMama baseline had general milestones, sleep and first-food articles, but neither of these dedicated routes. This step adds useful age-specific explanations and original observation aids. It does not copy competitor schedules, meal tables, proprietary predictions or performance claims.

BabyCenter's localized public app listings and Huckleberry's tracking page informed the existing product terminology research. Their full sites were not comprehensively crawled. A localized listing demonstrates wording in that listing; it does not prove that the installed app supports that language. No competitor rank, traffic, backlink, SEO-difficulty or AI-citation data was measured.

## English health sources and scope

- [AASM child sleep duration advisory](https://aasm.org/advocacy/position-statements/child-sleep-duration-health-advisory/): the age-scoped 4–12-month total is explicitly attributed. The example's times and arithmetic are original and are not a prescribed schedule from AASM.
- [AAP safe-sleep guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/sleep/Pages/A-Parents-Guide-to-Safe-Sleep.aspx): the sleep-environment paragraph links directly to the source. A log cannot establish environmental safety.
- [NHS first solid foods](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/weaning-and-feeding/babys-first-solid-foods/): the article attributes the around-six-month introduction and milk context to this guidance.
- [CDC introduction of solid foods](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/foods-and-drinks/when-what-and-how-to-introduce-solid-foods.html): readiness and food preparation are considered separately from age. The worksheet does not set intake targets or an allergen protocol.

## Demand evidence is narrower than the content decision

Prior all-location, language-selected Planner research provided ranges for the English, Persian, Arabic, Turkish and Portuguese age-topic seeds. The new French `diversification alimentaire 6 mois` and Spanish `alimentación complementaria 6 meses` submissions returned 1K–10K each. These are broader first-food intents, not proof of demand for a particular clock-time schedule. French and Spanish sleep phrases remained unmeasured after the interface removed them. Missing is not zero.

The eight submissions include repeated phrases from the earlier pilot. They must not be counted as eight unique additions. Planner ranges can group close variants, and ad competition is not organic search difficulty. No traffic forecast is calculated by adding the ranges.

## Product and publication boundaries

Product translations follow the previously verified client behavior: selected child profiles, nursing timing/side, bottle amount/unit, sleep/diaper records, reports, and actual free/paid boundaries. Nursing time is not described as measured milk volume. Public web tools are kept distinct from account-based app records. These are content changes, not newly implemented client-app features.

The health articles and their translations have source checks and automated/visual QA, but no independent pediatric or native-speaking editorial approval. Existing health articles outside this batch were not re-reviewed. There is no new medically-reviewed badge, credential or claim of complete market coverage.
